04 Lottie / TGS Colour Adapter
Import one or more Lottie .json or Telegram .tgs stickers and pick one or more target colours. In “Keep harmony” mode your first target replaces the design's main colour, the second replaces its next distinct colour, and so on; every remaining colour follows its nearest one, so relationships and contrast survive; “Monochrome” pushes everything to one hue. Black, white and grey stay untouched by default. Lock any colour to keep it original, or tap an adapted swatch to set your own. Import a whole sticker pack at once and adapt every file together.
